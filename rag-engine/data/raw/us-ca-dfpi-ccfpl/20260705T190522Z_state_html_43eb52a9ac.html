Financial Code - FIN

DIVISION 24. California Consumer Financial Protection Law [90000 - 90019]

  ( Division 24 added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 157, Sec. 7. )
  

CHAPTER 2. Short Title [90001- 90001.]
  ( Chapter 2 added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 157, Sec. 7. )

  
90001.  

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the “California Consumer Financial Protection Law.”

(Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 157, Sec. 7. (AB 1864) Effective January 1, 2021.)
